Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 26, 2026

1. Introduction

TeePals ("we", "our", or "us") operates the TeePals mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple ID, name (if shared), and email (if shared).
  • Profile information: First name, last name, nickname, city/location, birth date, gender, skill level, and profile photos you choose to upload.
  • Content you create: Rounds, posts, comments, chat messages, and photos you share within the app.
  • Location data: With your permission, we use your location to find golf rounds near you and set your home location.

We also collect information automatically:

  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, and push notification tokens.
  • Usage data: Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the TeePals service
  • Match you with nearby golf rounds and players
  • Send push notifications about round updates, messages, and social activity
  • Ensure trust and safety within the community
  • Improve and develop new features

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these cases:

  • With other users: Your public profile (nickname, city, photos, skill level) is visible to other authenticated users. Your birth date and email are never shared.
  • Service providers: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, data storage, and analytics. We use Google Places API for course photos.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure. We implement access controls to ensure only you can access your private data (such as birth date). All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • View and edit your profile information at any time within the app
  • Delete your account by contacting us at the email below
  • Opt out of push notifications via iOS Settings
  • Revoke location access via iOS Settings

7. Children's Privacy

TeePals is not intended for users under 17 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, contact us at:

Email: support@teepals.com
